# Changelog

## 1.0.1

- Fixes cleanup of the top bar indicator when the extension is disabled, as requested in the extensions.gnome.org review.

## 1.0.0

First release, for GNOME Shell 50.

- Countdown from the top bar menu: minute buttons that add or subtract, "At HH:MM", reminder
  message, Accept and Cancel.
- Alarms like GNOME Clocks: days of the week or once, name, own sound, ringing time and snooze.
- Top bar shows the time of the next alarm if it is today, the countdown's time left, or only
  the icon.
- Reminder as a dialog, a fading on-screen message or a system notification, with optional
  repeating sound. Reminders that ring together are shown one after the other.
- Alarms survive screen locks, suspend and restarts; missed ones are shown on return.
- English and Spanish.
